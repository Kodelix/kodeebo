                                        Executive Regulations
                                 of the Personal Data Protection Law

Article 1

For the purposes of these Regulations, the definitions set forth in the Personal Data Protection Law shall
have the same meaning herein. The term “Law” refers to Law No. 151 of 2020 on Personal Data
Protection.

  Policies, Procedures, Controls, and Standard Criteria for Collecting, Processing, Storing, and
                                     Securing Personal Data

Article 2

Personal Data collection, Processing, storage, and Security shall be carried out in accordance with the
following controls, standards, procedures, and policies:

First – Controls and Standards:

    1. The entity collecting Personal Data shall hold a License or Permit as a Controller or Processor,
       without prejudice to other obligations imposed by competent authorities regulating the activity.
    2. Personal Data shall not be collected except after obtaining the Data Subject’s consent and
       informing them clearly of the purpose. Providing Personal Data to receive lawful services or
       transactions constitutes consent by a natural person for collection and Processing of such data
       for that purpose only. Such data may not be used for any other purposes unless prior consent is
       obtained.
    3. Approval from the Personal Data Protection Center (“the Center”, or “PDPC”) shall be obtained
       for mechanisms used in Personal Data collection and for obtaining consent from the Data
       Subject or guardian thereof in case of children’s data.
    4. Define the retention period based on the purpose of collection.
    5. Maintain confidentiality and prohibit use, disclosure, or circulation except as legally permitted
       and in accordance with the License or Permit.

Second – Procedures and Policies:

    1. Inform the Data Subject of their rights under Article 2 of the Law.
    2. Implement security measures and software issued by the Center for Data protection, including
       devices and media used.
    3. Maintain a secure electronic register recording:
          - Data Subject’s consent, date thereof, and form thereof;
          - Description of the categories of Personal Data collected and the scope of use thereof.
          - Retention periods for each category, and the relation thereof to the intended purpose.
          - Regulatory and technical measures for Data Security enabling the Center to conduct
              periodic inspections and verify the compliance of the Licensee with such measures.

Policies, Procedures, Technical Controls, and Standards for the Obligations of the Personal Data
                                           Controller

Article 3

The obligations of the Personal Data Controller shall be in accordance with the following technical
standards, procedures, and policies:

First – Technical Standards and Controls:

    1. Obtain a License or Permit from the Center in accordance with the categories, conditions, and
       procedures specified in these Regulations, without prejudice to obligations imposed by
       competent authorities regulating the activity.
    2. Do not deviate from the licensed or permitted purpose for using Personal Data collected and
       processed under the License or Permit issued by the Center to the Controller.
    3. Verify that the Personal Data collected is accurate by reviewing its source, whether from
       employees or directly from the Data Subject, and ensure its consistency with the purpose of
       collection and Processing as per the License or Permit issued by the Center to the Controller.
    4. Erase Personal Data immediately upon the expiry of the retention purpose and notify the Data
       Subject of such erasure. Personal Data shall not remain in a form that allows identification of
       the Data Subject if retained for any lawful reason after the purpose has ended.
    5. Establish a mechanism approved by the Center enabling the Data Subject to request access to
       their Personal Data, withdraw prior consent, correct or amend their Data, restrict Processing
       within a defined scope, or object to any Processing.
    6. A Controller located outside the Arab Republic of Egypt and having no branch or representative
       office inside the country shall appoint a representative within Egypt through a company branch
       or an authorized office, approved by the Center for the duration of the License or Permit. If the
       Controller is a natural person, they shall appoint an agent within Egypt.
    7. Enable the Center inspectors, as judicial enforcement officers, to review electronic records and
       verify compliance with technical standards and security procedures for data protection, as well
       as any executive decisions issued by the Center in this regard.
    8. Comply with the volume and type of Personal Data permitted under the law governing its
       activity. Any additional Personal Data requested shall adhere to the rules and standards set forth
       in the Law, including retention, security, and transfer requirements, where such rules are absent
       in the governing law.
    9. Take necessary measures to ensure that individuals collecting Personal Data maintain
       confidentiality of such Personal Data and do not use, circulate, or disclose such Data in any form
       except for legally prescribed reasons.

Second – Procedures and Policies:
Al-Waqa'i' al-Misriyya - Issue No. 244, Supplement A, dated 1 November 2025


    1. Conduct periodic testing and evaluation to ensure the accuracy and integrity of collected
       Personal Data, in accordance with the assessment and inspection mechanisms issued by the
       Center.
    2. Implement measures to render Personal Data unreadable and ensure that such Data does not
       remain in a form that allows identification of the Data Subject when retained for legal reasons
       or national security considerations. Such Data shall be erased upon the expiry of the legal reason
       or purpose.
    3. Take technical measures necessary to maintain Data confidentiality and prevent breaches.
    4. Adopt technical and regulatory measures to ensure the ability to restore Personal Data, access it
       in a timely manner, and contain it in case of any physical or technical incident.
    5. Without prejudice to the obligation to maintain electronic records referred to in Article 2 of these
       Regulations, the Controller shall maintain secure electronic records that include:
            • Requests by the Data Subject to add or amend Personal Data, including the Data to be
                amended, whether the amendment was completed or not, and the reason therefor.
            • Requests by the Data Subject to erase Data or withdraw prior consent, including whether
                the erasure was completed or not and the method of notifying the Data Subject thereof.
            • Personal Data retained for legal reasons or national security considerations, enabling
                Center inspectors to verify compliance with technical and security standards without
                allowing identification of the Data Subject by third parties.

     Policies, Procedures, Controls, Conditions, Instructions, and Standard Criteria for the
                           Obligations of the Personal Data Processor

Article 4

The Processing of Personal Data shall be carried out in accordance with the following controls,
standards, procedures, and policies:

First – Controls and Standards:

    1. Obtain a License or Permit from the Center in accordance with the categories, conditions, and
       procedures specified in these Regulations, without prejudice to obligations imposed by
       competent authorities regulating the activity.
    2. Establish a mechanism approved by the Center that defines the volume of Personal Data and the
       purpose of Processing the same, and allows recording the Data Subject’s consent, as well as
       notifying the Controller, the Data Subject, and all concerned parties of the required Processing
       period.
    3. Ensure that employees handling Personal Data at the Processor maintain confidentiality and do
       not use, circulate, or disclose such Data in any form except for legally prescribed reasons.
    4. Enable the Center inspectors, as judicial enforcement officers, to review electronic records and
       verify compliance with technical standards and security procedures for data protection, as well
       as any executive decisions issued by the Center, and ensure that Processing purposes align with
       the nature of the licensed activity.
Al-Waqa'i' al-Misriyya - Issue No. 244, Supplement A, dated 1 November 2025


   5. A Processor located outside the Arab Republic of Egypt and having no branch or representative
      office inside the country shall appoint a representative within Egypt through a company branch
      or an authorized office, approved by the Center for the duration of the License or Permit. If the
      Processor is a natural person, they shall appoint an agent within Egypt.
   6. Prohibit Processing of any Personal Data for purposes other than those of the Controller or its
      activity, except for statistical or educational purposes that are non-profit, subject to the
      following conditions:

        a) Obtain the Data Subject’s consent.
        b) Ensure that the subject of the study is relevant to the Personal Data being processed.
        c) When Personal Data is circulated in any form, it shall be encoded so that the Data Subject
            cannot be identified.
   7. The Processor shall handle Personal Data during Processing and when using it for artificial
      intelligence training and emerging or innovative technologies in accordance with locally,
      regionally, and internationally recognized principles, ensuring that such technologies are used
      in a manner that does not cause harm to the Data Subject.
   8. The Processor shall comply with the volume and type of Personal Data permitted under the law
      governing its activity. Any additional Personal Data requested shall adhere to the rules and
      standards set forth in the Law, including retention, security, and transfer requirements, where
      such rules are absent in the governing law.

Second – Procedures and Policies:

   1. Implement security measures necessary to protect and secure Personal Data during Processing,
      including securing devices and media used, and store Personal Data in an unreadable format to
      ensure confidentiality and prevent unauthorized identification of the Data Subject.
   2. Adopt technical and regulatory measures that guarantee the ability to restore Personal Data,
      access it in a timely manner, and contain it in case of any physical or technical incident.
   3. Incorporate into operational policies the maintenance of a secure electronic register that
      includes:
          • Recording and describing Processing operations performed, as well as the categories of
               Personal Data used, and scope of use. The register shall include details of the Processor,
               a copy of the Processing contract concluded with the Controller, details of the
               Controller’s Data Protection Officer, details of the Controller’s legal representative,
               Processing standards, and—where data is transferred across borders—the countries to
               which data is transferred, security systems applied, data flow paths, and a general
               description of technical standards used for data protection.
          • The time periods required for Processing each category of Personal Data.
          • The regulatory and technical measures followed for securing data, Processing
               operations, and storage, enabling the Center to conduct periodic inspections and verify
               the Processor’s compliance.
Al-Waqa'i' al-Misriyya - Issue No. 244, Supplement A, dated 1 November 2025


            •   Recording the date and time of data erasure after Processing is completed, or
                confirmation of its delivery to the Controller, in accordance with legally prescribed
                circumstances.

   Obligations of the Controller and Processor in Cases of Personal Data Breach or Violation

Article 5

The Controller and Processor, as the case may be, shall notify the Center through its designated
electronic portal or hotline within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of any Personal Data
Breach or Violation. Such notification shall be recorded in a secure electronic register prepared for this
purpose and include the following:

    1. The time and date of awareness of the Personal Data Breach or Violation and the time of
       reporting.
    2. A description of the nature of the Personal Data Breach or Violation and the time of occurrence,
       enabling the Center to estimate the approximate volume of compromised data.
    3. The potential consequences of the Personal Data Breach or Violation and the expected extent of
       damage.
    4. Immediate measures and corrective actions taken in response to the Personal Data Breach or
       Violation .
    5. Details of the Data Protection Officer.
    6. Any additional documents, data, or information requested by the Center.

If the Personal Data Breach or Violation relates to national security considerations or the entities
responsible for them, the notification shall be made to the Center immediately and shall include, in
addition to the above, the following:

    a) The connection of the Personal Data Breach or Violation to national security considerations.
    b) The volume of data affected by the Personal Data Breach or Violation and an assessment of the
       resulting damage.

In all cases, the Controller and Processor, as the case may be, shall notify the Data Subject within three
(3) business days from the date of reporting the Personal Data Breach or Violation to the Center, along
with the security measures taken, using the agreed communication method (SMS, email, or phone call)
specified when consent for data collection was obtained.

             Obligations of the Center in Cases of Personal Data Breach or Violation

Article 6

The Center shall provide appropriate channels and means for reporting Personal Data Breaches or
Violations, while ensuring the adoption of a dedicated communication method for receiving reports
related to national security considerations.
Al-Waqa'i' al-Misriyya - Issue No. 244, Supplement A, dated 1 November 2025


The Center shall also coordinate with National Security Authorities to determine the mechanisms for
notifying them upon receiving reports of Personal Data Breaches or Violations.

Furthermore, the Center shall regularly train and raise awareness among Personal Data Protection
Officers on standards for classifying the nature of Personal Data Breaches or Violations.

                   Conditions for Registering Personal Data Protection Officers

Article 7

The following conditions shall be met for registering Personal Data Protection Officers:

    1. The applicant shall hold academic qualifications or professional certifications and have practical
       experience in relevant fields, in accordance with the standards adopted by the Center’s Board of
       Directors for the purpose of Personal Data protection.
    2. The applicant shall pass the tests approved by the Center, based on the nature and scale of the
       Personal Data activity for which registration is requested.
    3. The applicant shall not have been previously convicted of any crimes involving dishonor or
       breach of trust.

    Documents Required for Registration in the Personal Data Protection Officers’ Register

Article 8

The application for registration in the Personal Data Protection Officers’ Register shall be submitted
along with the following documents:

    1. A copy of the applicant’s identification document (National ID for Egyptians, passport for
       foreigners).
    2. A recent personal photograph.
    3. Copies of academic qualifications obtained.
    4. Details of practical experience in relevant fields.
    5. Criminal record certificate for Egyptians, and for foreigners duly authenticated by the competent
       authorities.
    6. Proof of passing the tests prescribed by the Center for registration.
    7. The Data Protection Officer code, if previously registered in the Center Personal Data Protection
       Officers’ Register for another Controller or Processor, or if previously registered as a natural
       person and now seeking registration with a Controller or Processor.

The Center shall review the application and notify the applicant of acceptance or rejection within thirty
(30) business days from the date of submission. The Center may request additional documents necessary
for decision-making within a specified period, and shall notify the applicant of acceptance or rejection
within fifteen (15) days from the date of completing the required documents.

The legal representative of any entity shall take the necessary steps to register Personal Data Protection
Officers to enable them to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of the Law.

                          Registration of Personal Data Protection Officers

Article 9

The Center shall establish an electronic register dedicated to the registration of Personal Data Protection
Officers. Each officer shall be assigned a unique identification number referred to as the “Personal Data
Protection Officer Code”, which shall be linked to the nature and volume of data they are authorized to
handle based on the results of their qualification tests. This code shall allow retrieval of all relevant
information about the officer.

Registration shall be completed through the Center’s electronic portal on the designated links by either
of the following methods:

    1. A request submitted by the legal representative of the Controller or Processor to register an
       employee in the Personal Data Protection Officers’ Register, including proof of meeting the
       registration requirements and the volume and nature of data the officer is authorized to handle.
    2. A request submitted by a natural person, including proof of meeting the registration
       requirements and the volume and nature of data they are authorized to handle.

The Personal Data Protection Officer Code shall be determined based on compliance with the
registration requirements.

 Termination of Contractual Relationship or Replacement of Personal Data Protection Officers

Article 10

The legal representative of any Controller or Processor, when intending to terminate the relationship
with a Personal Data Protection Officer, shall notify the Center at least fifteen (15) days prior to such
termination. During this period, the representative shall submit a request to register or assign another
Personal Data Protection Officer—whether from the entity’s organizational structure or under a
contractual arrangement—consistent with the nature and volume of data previously handled. The request
shall specify the code of the replacement officer and the duration of assignment if temporary. This shall
be done through the Center’s electronic portal or any other communication method approved by the
Center.

The Center may suspend a registered Personal Data Protection Officer and request their replacement if
they violate any registration conditions. In such cases, the legal representative shall register a temporary
replacement officer from those already registered with the Center, whether from the entity’s
organizational structure or under a contractual arrangement, for the same volume and nature of data
handled, until a permanent officer is appointed within a timeframe determined by the Center.
Al-Waqa'i' al-Misriyya - Issue No. 244, Supplement A, dated 1 November 2025


The legal representative shall also notify and provide the Center with the contact details of the
replacement officer.

                     Scope of Authority of the Personal Data Protection Officer

Article 11

A Personal Data Protection Officer registered with the Center may perform their duties within more than
one organizational structure, provided the following two conditions are met:

    1. The entities for which the Personal Data Protection Officer is registered shall consent to the
       officer performing duties for other entities or legal persons, without resulting in any conflict of
       interest, and within the limits of the volume and nature of data the Personal Data Protection
       Officer is authorized to handle.
    2. The Center shall approve the registration of the Personal Data Protection Officer for more than
       one entity, based on the nature and scale of such entities’ activities, and after ensuring that such
       registration does not create any conflict or impair the officer’s duties.

It is permissible to register a single Personal Data Protection Officer for entities that are structurally or
organizationally linked and whose activities are integrated through data exchange, provided the Center
is notified accordingly.

                         Obligations of the Personal Data Protection Officer

Article 12

The Personal Data Protection Officer shall comply with the following obligations:

    1. Monitor the implementation of security policies issued by the Center regarding the protection
       of Processing, storage, and circulation of Personal Data, and submit an annual report to the
       Center on the state of privacy protection at the Controller or Processor, or upon request.
    2. Submit a report to the Center within fifteen (15) days from assuming duties as a replacement
       Personal Data Protection Officer, detailing the state of privacy protection, in cases of
       replacement as provided under Article 10 of these Regulations.
    3. Oversee the handling of notifications and complaints submitted by Data Subjects regarding
       requests to erase, amend, or add Personal Data, and ensure their execution.
    4. Avoid any assignments that conflict with or compromise the protection of Personal Data.
    5. Establish a separate system when serving multiple entities, institutions, or companies, to
       facilitate the performance of duties and responsibilities and enable the Center to review
       compliance

                                             Digital Evidence

Article 13
Al-Waqa'i' al-Misriyya - Issue No. 244, Supplement A, dated 1 November 2025


Digital evidence derived from Personal Data shall have the same probative value as evidence obtained
from written data and information, provided that the following technical standards and conditions
are met:

    1. The collection or extraction of digital evidence related to Personal Data shall be carried out
       using technologies that ensure no alteration, update, deletion, or deviation in the Personal Data
       and related information.
    2. The digital evidence shall be relevant to the incident and within the scope of proving or
       disproving the matter in accordance with the mandate of the investigating authority or the
       competent court.
    3. The collection, extraction, and preservation of the evidence shall be performed by judicial
       enforcement officers authorized to handle this type of evidence or by experts from investigative
       or judicial authorities. The reports or technical records shall specify the type and specifications
       of the software, tools, and devices used, ensuring the original is preserved without tampering.
    4. Digital evidence shall be documented in a procedure report by the competent authority prior to
       examination and analysis, through printing copies of the stored files or scanning them by any
       visual or digital means. These copies shall be certified by those responsible for collecting,
       extracting, or analyzing the digital evidence, and each copy shall bear the date and time of
       printing or scanning, the name of the person performing the task, details of the devices,
       equipment, and tools used, and the data and information related to the content of the evidence.

                   Standards and Controls for Handling Sensitive Personal Data

Article 14

Each of the Controller or Processor, as the case may be, whether a natural or legal person, shall comply
with the following standards and controls when collecting, transferring, storing, retaining, Processing,
or Making Available Sensitive Personal Data:

    1. Obtain a License or Permit from the Center in accordance with the nature of its activity and the
       categories of Licenses and Permits specified in these Regulations.
    2. Obtain explicit written consent (in paper or electronic form) from the Data Subject or the
       guardian in the case of children’s data, except in cases authorized by law.
    3. Ensure that such data is essential and necessary for the specific purpose related to the
       Controller’s or Processor’s activity and that its use does not cause harm to the Data Subject.
    4. Comply with the security standards prescribed by the Center for handling Sensitive Personal
       Data.
    5. In cases where a child participates in a game, contest, or any other activity, no more data shall
       be collected than is necessary for such participation, and such data shall not be used for profiling,
       tracking, or behavioral monitoring of children.
    6. Any other standards adopted by the Center’s Board of Directors aimed at protecting Sensitive
       Personal Data.
    7. Maintain secure electronic records as required by the Center, including:
Al-Waqa'i' al-Misriyya - Issue No. 244, Supplement A, dated 1 November 2025


        a) Recording the consents of the Data Subject or the child’s guardian when handling Sensitive
           Personal Data in any of the forms mentioned.
        b) Recording requests for deletion, erasure, amendment, or suspension of Processing of
           Sensitive Personal Data submitted by the Data Subject or the child’s guardian, along with
           evidence of their implementation.

                       Standards and Controls for Handling Children’s Data

Article 15

Holders, Controllers, or Processors of data relating to children under the age of fifteen (15) shall obtain
explicit written consent (in paper or electronic form) from the guardian prior to collecting and Processing
such data for the purpose of providing a service or for any other purpose. The consent shall specify its
time frame, without prejudice to the guardian’s right to withdraw or amend their consent. The Center
shall approve the mechanisms and formats for such consents.

For children aged fifteen (15) to eighteen (18), the child or their guardian, as the case may be, shall
provide the guardian’s consent for the collection and Processing of the child’s data. The Center shall
determine the mechanisms for obtaining such consent from the guardian in a manner that ensures
compliance with the legal requirements prescribed in this regard.

Policies, Standards, Controls, and Rules for Transferring, Storing, Sharing, Processing, Making
                     Available, or Protecting Personal Data Across Borders

Article 16

The transfer, storage, sharing, Processing, or Making Available of Personal Data across borders shall be
subject to the following controls, rules, policies, and standards:

First – Controls and Rules:

    1. The Controller or Processor, as the case may be, shall obtain a License or Permit from the Center
       for transferring Personal Data collected or prepared for Processing to a foreign country for
       Processing, storage, or sharing purposes, based on the Center’s assessment of the adequacy of
       protection level in that country.
    2. The Controller or Processor, as the case may be, shall obtain the Data Subject’s consent for
       transferring Personal Data collected or prepared for Processing to a foreign country for
       Processing, storage, or sharing purposes.
    3. Each of the Controller or Processor shall take all necessary measures to ensure the use of
       technologies that guarantee an adequate level of protection for Personal Data during transfer,
       circulation, sharing, or storage, in accordance with the License or Permit issued by the Center
       and commensurate with the volume and nature of the data authorized for cross-border transfer,
       sharing, storage, or Processing.
Al-Waqa'i' al-Misriyya - Issue No. 244, Supplement A, dated 1 November 2025


    4. The Controller or Processor, as the case may be, shall transfer Personal Data collected or
       prepared for Processing only to the country or countries specified in the License or Permit issued
       by the Center and shall update the License or Permit if additional countries are added during its
       validity period.

Second – Policies and Standards:

The Center shall specify, in its policies, the countries that provide an adequate level of protection for
Personal Data in accordance with the provisions of the Law, without prejudice to establishing a
mechanism for periodic review, based on the following standards:

    1. The existence of legislation or regulations related to Personal Data protection and their
       consistency with the provisions of the Law.
    2. The availability of technical and security measures that ensure the protection of Personal Data.
    3. The existence of legal provisions for compensation in cases where the Data Subject suffers harm
       due to misuse of their Personal Data.

In light of the availability of the aforementioned standards, the Center may approve the issuance of a

       of Egypt.

Article 18

The sender, whether a Controller or Processor, of any electronic communication for the purpose of direct

First – Rules and Conditions:

    2. Obtain explicit consent from the Data Subject to receive marketing communications.

        a) If the Data Subject withdraws consent for the use of their data for Electronic Marketing
            purposes.
        b) Upon the expiry of the retention period or when the marketing purpose ceases, whichever

    1. Do not use Personal Data collected for Electronic Marketing for any other purpose, nor exchange

       purposes. The sender shall also retain the source of the Personal Data, including proof of
       consent; otherwise, they shall immediately cease using such data for Electronic Marketing .
    4. Maintain electronic records, to be made available to the Center upon request, including:
            a) How and when the Data Subject’s consent for Electronic Marketing was obtained, and
                the specific purpose.
            b) Requests for deletion or modification of such consent and the actions taken in that
                regard.
            c) Mechanisms for securing and storing Personal Data in accordance with procedures
                approved by the Center.

In all cases, the Center shall designate a means of communication to receive citizens’ complaints related
to direct Electronic Marketing, whether through its website or via dedicated short telephone numbers.
