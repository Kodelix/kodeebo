compile option SECURE_DELETE present and `PRAGMA secure_delete` = 1 by default, so the case named "default-delete-only"

sets `PRAGMA secure_delete=OFF` explicitly. Run 1's WAL, journal PERSIST/TRUNCATE results are not affected by this (they
concern files other than the main-file free pages) but secure_delete=1 was in force there too.
