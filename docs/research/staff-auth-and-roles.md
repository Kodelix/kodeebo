## 1. Question

How do people sign in to Kodeebo, and what may they do, on three surfaces (founder, 2026-10-19)?

1. **Kodelix operator console** — the founder only today; issues seats and licences and support grants; highest protection (licensing threat model TB3, M15: passkey/WebAuthn, step-up).
2. **Tenant dashboard** — a web PWA with a Play wrap, for owner and staff. Required: sign-in with username or email and password; forgot password (OTP by email or SMS); 2FA with TOTP optional and passkeys; profile; change password with the current password re-verified (ADR-0001 § 1c).
3. **Cashier app on the Windows counter** — local-first, often offline for days (up to the lease: 7 days base, 14 maximum): first sign-in online with username and password; then an alphanumeric PIN for the shift; fast user switching on a shared PC; a manager PIN override for sensitive actions; PIN reset by an authorised person, never by OTP.

**Roles (founder decision, 2026-10-19):** custom; the owner creates roles and chooses each role's permissions; owner, manager and cashier ship as editable templates; roles are scoped to branches by staff assignment (ADR-0003).

Decision areas: (A) dashboard and console sign-in; (B) account recovery; (C) cashier sign-in offline; (D) staff lifecycle offline; (E) the custom-role permission model and manager override; (F) the audit trail of sensitive actions. The founder decides; `cto` records the staff-auth ADR. **Desktop runtime** (A = Compose Desktop on Kotlin/JVM, B = Electron on TypeScript; undecided): areas A, B and E–F's rules do not depend on it; the cashier-side hash library, the DPAPI route and where the device's checks run do (sections 4.3, 4.5).

## 7. Performance & Caching Notes

- **Expected load:** dashboard sign-ins a few per day per venue; counter switches and overrides a few to a few dozen per shift (assumption, not sourced); recovery rare. The server's cost driver is Argon2id memory under concurrent or hostile sign-ins (cap concurrency, delay per account and IP).
- **Hot paths:** server — one `SessionLookup.find` plus the principal read per request (now a join through `roles` → `role_permissions`, unmeasured), no cache (ASVS 7.4.1, 8.3.2); sign-in one Argon2id verify (a dummy verify for unknown users). Device — one PIN verify per switch or override: 0.017–0.33 s on a Linux core across the fitted options (F213, F214); **4 GB Windows PC not measured (S22, S23): against the 3 s counter ceiling and `perf-budgets.md` (absent), not checked**; the hash must not run on the UI thread.
- **Cache decisions (gate 5):** no cache for sessions, step-up grants, WebAuthn challenges, OTPs, recovery codes or server-side authority (each must be revocable or single-use immediately). The device's staff table and authority snapshot are replicas keyed by `staff_version` / `authority_version`, replaced by the feed, not TTL caches; maximum age is the lease. The breached-password list is a static asset (source NOT SOURCEABLE).
- **Budgets affected:** sign-in and password-change p95 (`perf-budgets.md` absent), counter action latency (3 s), sync feed freshness, audit rows in sync batches, the activity-trail list index `(tenant_id, branch_id, occurred_at)`.
