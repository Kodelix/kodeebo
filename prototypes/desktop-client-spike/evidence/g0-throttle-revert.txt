## Result vs plan (±20%)
random 4K: 97.07 IOPS vs 100 -> PASS
sequential 1M: 11.76 MiB/s read, 11.87 MiB/s write vs 80 MiB/s -> FAIL
Finding G0-F2: QEMU's IOPS cap counts every request whatever its size, and the guest sends large I/O as about 128 KiB requests (1 MiB and 128 KiB blocks both give ~96 I/O per s, ~12 MiB/s; 64 KiB gives ~94 I/O per s, 5.9 MiB/s). An IOPS + bandwidth throttle therefore cannot make random I/O HDD-slow and sequential I/O HDD-fast at once: with 100 IOPS, sequential is ~12 MiB/s (slower than a real HDD); to reach 80 MiB/s sequentially, random 4K would rise to ~640 IOPS (faster than a real HDD). The 128 KiB request size is inferred from the matching numbers, not read from a driver setting.
